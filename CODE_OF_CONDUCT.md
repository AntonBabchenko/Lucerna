# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Lucerna project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity.

## Our standard

Lucerna adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its Code of Conduct.
The full canonical text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>, with
translations at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations>.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and help keep the
project a place where people are glad to take part. Conduct that makes others feel
unwelcome or unsafe has no place here.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainer at
**anton.babchenko@outlook.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and
the maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
